# Process Adjunct, Not Truth

Tool/prompt versions, build fingerprints, runtime bytes and transport hashes are process metadata, not business truth. Closeout, recovery and continuation depend on business obligations, actual effects and safe retryability, using the current implementation. Missing or changed process metadata alone must not stop progress. Retain genuine authorization, tenancy, identity and effect protection at the boundaries where needed.
